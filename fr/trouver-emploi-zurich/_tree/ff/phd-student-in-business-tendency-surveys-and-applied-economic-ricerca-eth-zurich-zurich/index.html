PhD Student in Business Tendency Surveys and Applied Economic Research

100%, Zurich, fixed-term

KOF Swiss Economic Institute at ETH Zurich is Switzerland’s leading academic institute for applied economic research. KOF combines independent, policy-relevant research with regular business tendency surveys, economic analysis and forecasting. It offers a collaborative research environment in which academic research and applied economic work reinforce each other. The Business Tendency Surveys Business Tendency Surveys division at KOF is looking for a PhD researcher in applied economics who is interested in combining academic research with ongoing economic analysis.

Project background

The Business Tendency Surveys division is responsible for designing, conducting and analysing surveys of firms across a broad range of sectors of the Swiss economy. The resulting indicators provide timely information on economic conditions and expectations and are widely used in economic analysis and forecasting.

Alongside its survey activities, the division conducts empirical research on firm expectations, behaviour and economic developments. Its survey data and construction-sector forecasting could provide opportunities for day-to-day analysis and PhD research to inform and reinforce each other.

The starting date is flexible. The appointment is initially for 18 months, with extensions envisaged subject to satisfactory progress, up to a total of six years.

Job description

You will join the Business Tendency Surveys team and analyse survey results, particularly for the Swiss construction sector and architectural and engineering services. You will also pursue a PhD in applied economics, aiming for research that draws on and contributes to the division’s ongoing work. Research topics will be developed with the supervisory team.

Your responsibilities will include

  • Analysing and interpreting results from KOF’s business tendency surveys, including those covering construction and related services.
  • Monitoring and forecasting developments in the Swiss construction sector.
  • Contributing to the KOF Economic Forecast in collaboration with researchers from other divisions.
  • Representing KOF in Euroconstruct, an international network engaged in the analysis and forecasting of European construction markets.
  • Conducting original empirical research as part of a PhD, addressing questions that either arise from, or inform, KOF’s survey, sectoral analysis and forecasting activities.

Profile

  • A Master’s degree (or equivalent) in economics or a related quantitative field, such as data science or statistics, with a solid foundation in economics.
  • A strong interest in empirical research related to business tendency surveys, firm expectations, sectoral economic developments or forecasting.
  • An interest in developing expertise in the Swiss construction sector and related services.
  • An interest in communicating economic analysis and research findings, both in writing and through graphical presentations.
  • Strong motivation to pursue a PhD in applied economics and the ability to develop and conduct original empirical research that both draws on and contributes to the division’s ongoing work, independently and in collaboration with other researchers.
  • Strong quantitative skills and experience with statistical programming, ideally in R (used for regular KOF work); knowledge of Python or Stata is also welcome.
  • Excellent German and very good English language skills.

We support the career of women in economics and we encourage female candidates to apply.

We offer

We offer a stimulating and collaborative research environment in which you are expected to pursue and complete a PhD at ETH Zurich under the supervision of Prof. Jan-Egbert Sturm, subject to formal admission to ETH Zurich’s doctoral programme. Your doctoral research should build on and complement your ongoing work at KOF, creating synergies between academic research and applied economic analysis.
