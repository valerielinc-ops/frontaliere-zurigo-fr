Description originale

  • Your role: As an intern, you will support the evaluation and development of sorbents and sorbent structures for DAC. You will gain hands-on experience in lab-based testing, data analysis, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

  • Material Stability: You investigate material stability by testing under accelerated aging conditions to simulate long-term performance.
  • Sorbent Characterization: You characterize sorbent behaviour by measuring unary and binary isotherms under controlled conditions.

  • Method Development: You contribute to the development, refinement, and validation of experimental methods to ensure reliable and reproducible testing outcomes.
  • Documentation & Communication: You validate, troubleshoot, and clearly document your work to ensure effective communication across interdisciplinary development teams at Climeworks.
