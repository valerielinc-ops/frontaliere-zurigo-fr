Description complète

Department of Mathematical Modeling and Machine Learning Start of employment as soon as possible, 2 years, extendable by 1–2 years, temporary The Quantitative Network Science group at the University of Zurich is hiring two postdoctoral researchers for the SNSF-funded project “Cross-platform, cross-national investigation of the impact of social media on civic discourse.” The project is led by Prof. Alexandre Bovet, in partnership with Prof. Frank Esser (Department of Communication and Media Research, UZH) and Prof. Laia Castro (Department of Political Science, University of Barcelona). The project Online social media platforms have become central spaces for political debate and civic participation. However, it is still unclear what most shapes the quality of that debate: platform design, who self-selects onto each platform, the emotional tone of news content, or national context. Most studies look at one platform in one country. Experiments run inside platforms have found little effect of design on attitudes, which seems to contradict observational evidence, partly because those experiments measure individuals rather than collective behavior. This project is a large-scale comparison of civic discourse across Germany, France, Spain and Hungary and five major platforms. These countries have different media systems, democratic traditions and levels of affective polarization. Using public data accessed under the EU Digital Services Act, we will study how similar news content produces different discourse outcomes across platforms and countries. The goals are to determine whether platform design or national context matters more, identify the mechanisms linking design to discourse quality, and test how far platform effects generalize.

Your responsibilities

Position 1: Measuring civic discourse (ML/NLP)

  • Develop and validate machine learning and LLM-based methods to measure the quality of civic discourse and the emotional framing of news content, across platforms, languages and modalities (text, images, video)
  • Build multilingual measurement pipelines covering German, French, Spanish and Hungarian content

Position 2: Comparative design, causal inference and surveys

  • Develop and validate the statistical matching framework for comparing discourse across platforms and countries
  • Design and run surveys to measure the demographic composition of platform audiences

Both positions

  • Collect and analyze public social media data on elections and other civic issues under the DSA
  • Formulate and test hypotheses about the mechanisms linking platform design to discourse quality
  • Work closely with communication and political scientists in Zurich and Barcelona
