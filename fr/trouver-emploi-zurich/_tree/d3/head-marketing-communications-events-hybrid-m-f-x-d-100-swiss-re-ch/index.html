Description complète

Are you a strategic events leader with a passion for bringing people together and delivering high-quality experiences? Do you combine creative thinking with strong execution skills, a keen eye for detail and a commitment to excellence? Reporting to the Head L&H Re Marketing & Communications, you will be part of the Communications, Public Affairs & SRI division. With a global remit spanning three business units and Public Affairs, you will drive alignment, consistency and excellence in event planning and delivery across business areas and regions. About the Role As Head Marketing & Communications Events at Swiss Re, you will lead a team of events professionals and oversee the planning and delivery of our global events portfolio. From flagship Swiss Re events to industry conferences and trade shows, you will work closely with business and marketing teams to translate their priorities into high-quality event experiences that strengthen relationships with clients, partners and key industry stakeholders, while supporting Swiss Re's business objectives and brand positioning. Key Responsibilities

  • Lead and develop a small global team of events professionals, providing direction, coaching and support while fostering a culture of collaboration, accountability and continuous improvement.
  • Partner with business, Marketing, Communications and Public Affairs stakeholders to translate strategic priorities into effective event strategies and plans that drive client engagement, strengthen market positioning and enhance Swiss Re's brand visibility.
  • Establish and oversee global event management processes, governance frameworks and common standards, including structured event intake and prioritisation, to ensure consistent, efficient and high-quality delivery across the organisation.
  • Develop scalable solutions, such as event-in-a-box, that enable business units to deliver events independently while maintaining operational rigour, quality standards and a cohesive brand experience.
  • Oversee the end-to-end planning, coordination and delivery of a diverse global portfolio of external events, including Swiss Re-owned flagship events, major industry conferences and trade shows.
  • Build and foster a global event management community across business units and regions, promoting knowledge sharing, capability development and the consistent adoption of global event standards and best practices.
  • Proactively shape and evolve Swiss Re's global events portfolio, challenging existing formats, identifying new opportunities and introducing innovative approaches, technologies and experiences that respond to changing business needs and audience expectations.
  • Oversee event budgets, events agencies and external vendor relationships, ensuring effective resource allocation, cost efficiency and high-quality delivery.
  • Establish and oversee event risk management, compliance and contingency planning standards, ensuring operational resilience and adherence to relevant corporate policies and requirements across the global events portfolio.
  • Collaborate with business and marketing stakeholders to define meaningful event performance measures, evaluate outcomes and use feedback and insights to continuously improve event effectiveness and business impact.

About the Team You will join Swiss Re's global Communications, Public Affairs & SRI division, working at the intersection of business strategy, reputation and stakeholder engagement. Our team partners closely with Business Unit leadership and colleagues across functions and regions to deliver integrated, business-focused marketing and communications solutions. We combine strategic expertise, creativity and commercial understanding to strengthen Swiss Re's market positioning and support client engagement. You will play an active role in shaping a more integrated, collaborative and strategically aligned Marketing & Communications organisation that delivers meaningful business impact.

About You

You are an accomplished leader with strong strategic mindset and a hands-on approach. You bring a wealth of experience in events management and know how to balance the big picture with the finer details. You are a natural collaborator who builds strong relationships across all levels of an organisation, and you have a proven ability to influence and engage stakeholders. You are passionate about creating exceptional experiences and are energised by the challenge of continuously raising the bar.

We are looking for candidates who bring

  • Proven experience in leading and developing an events team, with a strong track record in planning and delivering large-scale, high-profile events.
  • Extensive experience in corporate and/or industry event management, including flagship events, trade shows, conferences and client engagement events in an international environment.
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with senior leaders and cross-functional teams to translate business priorities into successful events.
  • Strong organisational and operational management skills, with a structured, process-driven approach and a track record of establishing effective governance, scalable processes and efficient ways of working.
  • Strong budget and vendor management skills, with experience overseeing significant event budgets, negotiating contracts and ensuring cost-effective, high-quality delivery.
  • Good understanding of event management technologies and digital platforms with the ability to work confidently across different systems, understand how data & platforms connect, and identify opportunities to improve event planning, delivery and efficiency.
  • An analytical and data-driven mindset, with experience measuring event performance, evaluating outcomes and using insights to drive continuous improvement.
  • Fluency in English, both written and spoken.

Additional experience that would be an advantage

  • Familiarity with the reinsurance or broader insurance industry landscape and key industry events
  • Experience with digital and hybrid event formats and the technology platforms that support them
  • Previous experience operating in a global matrix organisation, collaborating across business units, functions and regions.
  • Multilingual skills — German and/or French would be a distinct advantage given the Swiss Re context
