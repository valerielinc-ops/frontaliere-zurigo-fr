Entreprise et contexte

  • Demonstrable mastery of VC modeling objects: characteristics, classes, configuration profiles, object dependencies, constraints, variant tables, and their interplay in super-BoM and variant-BoM structures.
  • University degree in Data Management, Information Technology, Supply Chain, Engineering, Finance, or a related discipline.
  • Explicit experience owning BoM data strategy and mastership across PLM, ERP, and MES boundaries — engineering-BoM, manufacturing-BoM, and the change-propagation standards that keep them synchronized.
  • Strong understanding of data modelling, data architecture, and integration patterns.
  • Familiarity with modern data-management disciplines: data governance, data quality, data lineage, metadata management, data cataloguing, data observability, and data-mesh / data-fabric concepts.
