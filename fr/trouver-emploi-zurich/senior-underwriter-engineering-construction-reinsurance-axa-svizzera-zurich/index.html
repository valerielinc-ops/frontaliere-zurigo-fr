(Senior) Underwriter Engineering/Construction – Reinsurance (80-100%) Zurich, Switzerland

As an Underwriter within our Engineering & Construction team, you will play a vital role in assessing and underwriting a broad range of construction and engineering treaties.

Depending on your experience, this position offers an excellent opportunity to contribute at both a technical and strategic level—whether you are an accomplished professional or an emerging leader looking to further develop your expertise.

Responsabilités principales

  • For more experienced candidates, there will be opportunities to mentor junior underwriters, contribute to process improvements, and take on leadership responsibilities in underwriting initiatives. What you’ll be doing
  • What will your essential responsibilities include?
  • Support the underwriting of construction and engineering treaties through independent analysis and comprehensive risk assessment of client submissions, including understanding risk structures and exposures.
  • Develop and formulate quotations, pricing, and underwriting approval documents, ensuring prompt processing of slips and policy wordings.
  • Help grow and maintain a profitable in-force book by identifying new opportunities and effectively managing renewals.
  • Collaborate closely with team members, actuarial functions, client advocates, market leaders, and operational units to ensure cohesive and effective underwriting processes.
  • Conduct risk analysis considering cedent exposures, lines of business, and geographic factors; identify and evaluate exposures to ensure adherence to limits and risk management frameworks.
  • Contribute to the development and enhancement of exposure management and reporting tools, supporting the ongoing improvement of risk monitoring processes.
  • Utilize actuarial tools and work collaboratively with regional and global teams to analyze, assess, and price risks accurately.
  • Assist in producing comprehensive and accurate data reports, supporting strategic decision-making.
