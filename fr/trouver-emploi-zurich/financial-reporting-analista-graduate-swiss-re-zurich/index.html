Responsabilités principales

  • Key Responsibilities
  • Own and coordinate quarterly financial reporting processes, partnering with Group Finance and regional finance teams across multiple geographies
  • Analyse and interpret L&H financial performance data, providing clear and insightful explanations of variances against internal and external targets
  • Consolidate financial inputs from a diverse range of stakeholders, ensuring accuracy, consistency, and timeliness of reporting outputs
  • Contribute to external L&H disclosures and internal management reporting, supporting the delivery of high-quality analysis used for strategic steering purposes
  • Identify opportunities for process improvement within reporting workflows, driving efficiency and quality enhancements
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support ad hoc financial analyses and special projects as required by senior management About the Team
  • We are a vibrant, multinational team based in Zürich, working closely with specialists around the world.
  • Our team holds ownership of the consolidated L&H results, with a sharp focus on both external disclosures and internal management reporting.
  • We pride ourselves on delivering high-quality, actionable analysis to senior management that directly supports strategic decision-making.

Processus de candidature

  • If you hold a Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD and have up to three years of professional experience, apply for our 18-month Graduate Programme starting on 1 September 2027.
  • Knowledge of or strong academic exposure to IFRS accounting standards, with an understanding of how these apply within financial reporting contexts
  • Please note: The application process will remain open until at least October 18th.
  • We greatly appreciate every application and carefully review each one.
  • As a result, the selection process may take some time, but please rest assured that your application is being given thorough consideration.
  • We aim to provide an update on your application by November 16th at the latest.
  • Candidates selected for the final stage will be invited to our Assessment Centre on Friday, November 27th, preceded by an informal networking dinner with Swiss Re representatives on the evening before.
  • Please ensure you are available for both events.

Quel salaire propose Swiss Re pour ce poste ?

Swiss Re indique CHF 113 000 - 198 500 bruts par an pour ce poste à Zürich. Est-ce un poste à temps plein, et quel type de contrat propose Swiss Re ?

Le type de contrat affiché ici provient directement de l'annonce originale de l'employeur ; confirmez toujours les horaires exacts, le préavis et la durée d'essai avec Swiss Re pendant le processus de candidature, ces détails pouvant varier même au sein de la même catégorie de contrat.

Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Zurich ?

Les résidents UE/AELE domiciliés dans la zone frontalière du pays voisin du canton de Zurich peuvent demander un permis G ; l'employeur suisse le dépose auprès de l'office cantonal des migrations après signature du contrat. Les règles de zone frontalière et les délais varient selon le pays voisin et le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Zurich ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.
