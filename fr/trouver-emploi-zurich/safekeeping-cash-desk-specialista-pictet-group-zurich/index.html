Responsabilités principales

  • Your responsibilities include the following:
  • Manage cash operations, including withdrawals, deposits and currency exchange for the Zurich and Basel branches
  • Oversee the central fund and maintain currency stock levels as well as ensure smooth operation and refilling of ATM terminals
  • Handle the physical securities, precious metals, coins, cheques and coordinate money transport
  • Manage safe deposit boxes for clients and employees, including opening, closing and maintenance
  • Provide administrative and secretarial support, including mail handling, manage office supplies and coordinating gifts
  • Proactively organize and coordinate a variety of engaging internal and external events

Exigences principales

  • Two to three years’ experience in banking, ideally with cash operations, managing central funds as well as handling physical securities
  • Experience and genuine enthusiasm for working with high-end clientele
