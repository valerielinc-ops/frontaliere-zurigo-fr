Processus de candidature

  • ANYbotics is transforming industrial plants in the energy, process, and utility sectors by introducing autonomous, high-mobility robots to novel, challenging applications.
  • As we continue to scale the capabilities of the ANYmal platform, we are seeking a Product Manager for Physical AI.
  • In this role, you will be the bridge between our commercial goals, our strategic product vision and our world-class robotics engineering teams.
  • You will act as the "engine room" for the product, deeply integrating with teams focused on perception, navigation, behavior, and locomotion to ensure our autonomy roadmap is not only technically feasible but also pushes the boundaries of what is possible in the field and moves the needle to meet the evolving demands of our key industrial customers. Your Contributions

Entreprise et contexte

  • Roadmap Execution : Partner closely with the VP Robotics and the Product organization to translate strategic portfolio goals into detailed technical roadmaps.
  • Take ownership of the "how" and "when" for prioritizing, communicating and ensuring successful deployment improvements across perception, behavior, navigation, and locomotion.
  • Commercial Value Driven : Integrate key account insights into the product roadmap to align development priorities with customer business value and drive key metrics
  • Autonomy Strategy : Define and manage the detailed product strategy to improve Resilience & Reliability and Coverage.
  • Create clear value propositions to move the metrics for key customers and turn those into requirements including the Operational Design Domain (ODD) to guide engineering efforts and ensure resilient and robust robot behavior in complex industrial environments.
  • Cross-Functional Technical Leadership : Work daily with R&D teams (Perception, Navigation, Locomotion) to translate product needs into functional specifications.
  • Act as the primary product point of contact, ensuring that engineering trade-offs are balanced against customer value.
  • Performance Optimization : Drive the continuous improvement of robot reliability and safety.
  • Establish KPIs for robot performance (e.g., success rates, intervention frequency, navigation efficiency) and lead efforts to identify and remediate bottlenecks.
  • Technical Risk Management : Anticipate technical challenges and edge cases within the autonomy stack.
