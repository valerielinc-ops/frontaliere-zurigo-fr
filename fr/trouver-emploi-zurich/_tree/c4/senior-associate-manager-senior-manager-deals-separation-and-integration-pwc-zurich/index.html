Your Team

You will be part of the Deals Strategy and Operations team, focusing on identifying, planning and realizing value from an M&A transaction. We help clients with complex M&A related projects, locally as well internationally. You will support our deals practice, uniquely positioned as a leader in Switzerland, offering a full range of M&A consulting services, from strategy to execution, giving you a wide range of project opportunities. You can find more information here: https://www.pwc.ch/en/services/deals/separation-and-integration.html Zurich As soon as possible 100% Advisory Everyone welcome ID: 776097WD

Your Impact

  • Lead high-impact consulting projects in a timely manner for key industry players and mid market clients, build and maintain trusted internal and external relationships.
  • Deliver integration & separation (carve-out) readiness assessments and assist client in the preparation for Day-1 and Day-100.
  • Design and implement target operating models and develop the separation or integration plan to realise full deal value potential.
  • Execute separation and integration projects, often with high complexity and in multinational environments.
  • Bring strong project management capabilities, as well as functional perspectives (e.g. Commercial, back-office functions, IT, Operations).
  • Work in a friendly and supportive work environment, non-hierarchical and committed to providing you the professional development opportunities you seek.

Your Skill Set

  • Masters' degree and a minimum of 5-9 years relevant work experience, having worked primarily on deal and/or consulting-related assignments at a top-tier consulting firm or a Big4, or were part of the in-house deal team of a larger corporate.
  • Strong experience along the M&A lifecycle, in separation and/or integration projects and it is advantageous if you have deep insights into business functions (e.g. Technology/IT, HR, Supply Chain, Finance).
  • Experience in various of the following areas: Leading (a part of) an integration or separation programme, experience in planning and executing integration and separations, preparing for Day-1 readiness, developing interim and end-state target operating model, running a Project Management Office (IMO or SMO).
  • Comfortable advising clients across a wide variety of industries. Having a strong track record in a certain industry is a plus. Additionally, you feel comfortable in interpreting financial statements.
  • Eager to actively contribute to the growth of our Deals Strategy & and Operations team by leveraging your entrepreneurial spirit and personal network.
  • Strong analytical skills, and you are fluent in German and English.

Les délais varient selon le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Zurich ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.
