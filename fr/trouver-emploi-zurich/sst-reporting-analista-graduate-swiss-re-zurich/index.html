Responsabilités principales

  • Your responsibilities will include:
  • Contribute to the preparation and calculation of Swiss Re’s available capital and its expected change under SST for Swiss-domiciled legal entities and the Group.
  • Support the production of the annual SST report submitted to FINMA, as well as the Financial Condition Report reported externally.
  • Collaborate closely with teams across Finance, Asset Management, Treasury, Finance IT, and Risk Management.
  • Analyze key drivers and prepare insights for Finance stakeholders and business partners
  • Execute consistency checks and ensure completeness of controls.
  • Oversee the reconciliation of IFRS and SST balance sheets
  • Look for opportunities to standardize and automate reporting processes
  • Contribute to finance-related projects that strengthen operational efficiency, controls, and business resilience
  • Support the team in maintenance and development of financial codes for underlying calculations About the Team
