Description complète

Your role Interested in working in finance, but not sure where to start? Do you want to work for a firm with a truly global footprint? We're looking for ambitious students to join our 2027 Internship Program within our Wealth Management Switzerland team.

You’ll get to

  • build an understanding of the wealth management industry as well as our culture and strategy
  • support our front organization in their work with a goal of increasing client satisfaction
  • coordinate projects with various stakeholders and acquire know-how in project management and business development

Your team You’ll be working in our Business Management team in Zurich in the business area of Wealth Management Switzerland. This area is responsible for advising wealthy high net worth private individuals, families, managers & executives, entrepreneurs as well as lawyers, fiduciaries and notaries in Switzerland. The team supports the front organization across business management tasks and strategic projects, including Run the Bank and Change the Bank initiatives, senior management presentations, governance and leadership activities, learning and development initiatives, and ad hoc sales, growth and finance topics.

Your expertise

We’re looking for a candidate who

  • has completed at least 4 semesters of a bachelor's degree, and graduated no longer than 12 months ago (bachelor's or master's degree)
  • has a competitive minimum GPA of 4.5 according to the Swiss grading system
  • has values that align with ours: hard-working, trustworthy, dedicated and collaborative
  • is a strategic thinker with strong communication skills
  • is motivated to work in a business with high demands and tight deadlines
  • can evidence how experimental and responsible use of AI can improve outcomes and efficiency
  • is fluent in German and has good English skills

Start date: July 1st, 2027

Your program This paid internship will last six months. You’ll work with our experts and learn about the industry, our clients and our firm. You’ll be right at the heart of our business, learning from your colleagues, taking part in day-to-day operations. You'll be given mentors along the way and will have the chance to get to know some of the most senior members of our teams.

You’ll have plenty of opportunities to develop new skills and make contacts along the way. If you like challenges, enjoy learning and want to work where no two days are the same, this program is for you.

If you have a successful internship, you may get an offer to return for our Graduate Talent Program after you've completed your studies.

We also provide diversified asset management solutions and focused investment banking capabilities. Headquartered in Zurich, Switzerland, UBS is present in more than 50 markets around the globe. Le type de contrat affiché ici provient directement de l'annonce originale de l'employeur ; confirmez toujours les horaires exacts, le préavis et la durée d'essai avec UBS pendant le processus de candidature, ces détails pouvant varier même au sein de la même catégorie de contrat.

Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Zurich ?

Les citoyens UE/AELE résidant dans un État UE/AELE peuvent demander un permis G et travailler dans toute la Suisse : les zones frontalières ne s'appliquent plus à eux, et ils doivent rentrer au moins une fois par semaine. L'employeur suisse dépose la demande auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Zurich après signature du contrat. Les délais varient selon le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Zurich ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.
