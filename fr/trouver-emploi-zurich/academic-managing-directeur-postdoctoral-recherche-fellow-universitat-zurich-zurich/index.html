Responsabilités principales

  • Your responsibilities
  • The ITSL offers the opportunity to conduct research on complex questions of utmost societal importance and contemporary relevance.
  • Working closely with other research colleagues, you will contribute to ongoing and future research projects which address key legal challenges of the digital society.
  • The role combines research, academic leadership and teaching responsibilities. Your duties will include:
  • contributing to, leading and developing research projects;
  • publishing research in academic journals and presenting your work at conferences;
  • contributing to the preparation of expert reports and commissioned research studies;
  • teaching undergraduate and graduate courses.
  • Your research agenda will also be supported, in particular development of a Habilitation project.

Exigences principales

  • a first-class master's degree in law, ideally from a Swiss university; a distinguished doctoral degree (PhD or equivalent);
  • excellent written and spoken German and English;
  • the ability to work independently, efficiently and with attention to detail; a strong interest in information and communications law;
  • enthusiasm for teamwork in new research topics and complex legal questions;

Universität Zürich indique CHF 124'000 - 173'000 bruts par an pour ce poste à Zürich. Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Zurich ?

Les résidents UE/AELE domiciliés dans la zone frontalière du pays voisin du canton de Zurich peuvent demander un permis G ; l'employeur suisse le dépose auprès de l'office cantonal des migrations après signature du contrat. Les règles de zone frontalière et les délais varient selon le pays voisin et le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Zurich ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.
