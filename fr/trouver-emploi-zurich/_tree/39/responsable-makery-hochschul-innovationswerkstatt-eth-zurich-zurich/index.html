Head of MAKERY: Hochschul-Innovationswerkstatt

80%-100%, Zurich, fixed-term

We inspire and empower students to cultivate a maker and innovator mindset. Through hands-on projects, students gain valuable experience and develop competencies needed to tackle future challenges. Our interdisciplinary team supports students from all disciplines in exploring ideas, building prototypes, and bringing projects to life. We provide inspiring facilities, makerspaces, and equipment, along with expert support.

For a new initiative of UTL, we're now looking for a Head of MAKERY to join our team.

Project background

You will lead the development and operation of " MAKERY – The Makerspace for Education" .

This initiative strengthens hands-on, project-based learning in lectures across ETH Zurich by connecting technical infrastructure, educational expertise, and makerspace resources across the university.

You will be based at the new makerspace hub on the ETH Zentrum campus while also contributing to makerspace activities and collaborations across ETH Zurich.

  • We are establishing a new makerspace hub on the ETH Zentrum campus as part of a growing ETH-wide ecosystem.
  • We work closely with students, lecturers, and technical staff from diverse disciplines.
  • We support project-based learning activities throughout the academic year.
  • We take an agile and collaborative approach to developing and improving our services.
  • You will help shape a new ETH-wide initiative with lasting impact on teaching and learning.

Job description

As Head of MAKERY, you will be responsible for the following

  • Lead: You'll drive the strategic vision and development of MAKERY, ensuring its long-term success and alignment with ETH Zurich's educational priorities.
  • Manage: You'll lead and develop a new team of Makerspace Managers and foster collaboration across the MAKERY ecosystem.
  • Coordinate: You'll build partnerships across departments and central ETH units, represent MAKERY in institutional bodies and align stakeholders around shared objectives.
  • Govern: You'll establish standards for operations, access, safety, training and quality management while ensuring efficient and scalable processes.
  • Plan: You'll oversee resource planning, budgeting and investment decisions to support the sustainable growth of MAKERY.
  • Measure: You'll monitor performance, assess educational impact and provide data-driven recommendations for continuous improvement.
  • Build: You'll identify new opportunities, services and partnerships that strengthen hands-on learning across ETH Zurich.

Within the team, we work collaboratively and jointly create an inspiring environment for learning, innovation and experimentation.

Profile

We need your energy, leadership and expertise to help build and lead MAKERY:

  • You have completed a Master's degree, PhD or equivalent qualification in engineering, natural sciences, education, innovation management or a related field.
  • You have several years of experience leading complex interdisciplinary projects, programmes or organisational initiatives.
  • You have leadership experience and enjoy supporting and developing teams and individuals.
  • You have strong stakeholder management skills, are comfortable working across organisational boundaries and disciplines, and know how to collaborate with senior level stakeholders.
  • You have experience in strategic planning, organisational development, project management and change processes.
  • You understand the value of project-based learning and hands-on education. Experience in higher education and/or educational innovation is an advantage.
  • You are familiar with makerspace environments and have practical knowledge of technologies and equipment such as digital fabrication, rapid prototyping, 3D printing, laser cutting, electronics, CAD and related maker technologies.
  • You enjoy bringing together people, ideas and resources to create impactful solutions and sustainable organisations.
  • You combine strategic thinking with a pragmatic and collaborative approach to implementation.
  • You can communicate effectively in German and English.

A note: research indicates that women and individuals from marginalized groups tend to apply only when they meet all of the criteria. If you believe you are qualified but do not necessarily meet every criterion, we encourage you to apply.
