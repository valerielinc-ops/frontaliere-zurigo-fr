Description complète

Development Project Ownership

  • Project leadership: You define, lead, and interpret test campaigns and modelling activities to advance sorbent structure development and optimisation.
  • Technical ownership : You act as the technical owner for selected development projects, translating strategic goals into structured plans and work packages.
  • External partnerships: You collaborate with technology partners, research institutions, and suppliers to accelerate innovation and development.
  • Cross-functional collaboration: You work closely with sorbent, manufacturing, characterisation, and industrialisation teams to support technology maturation and informed decision-making.
  • Method development: You develop and apply analytical methods, characterisation techniques, and modelling tools to enhance development efficiency and performance.

Operational and Laboratory Responsibilities

  • Sorbent structures: You prepare, fabricate, and characterise new and existing sorbent structures to support development and testing campaigns from laboratory to pilot scale.
  • Technical insights: You execute experimental work, analyse data, and communicate findings through clear documentation, technical reports, and presentations.
  • Traceability: You maintain organised and reliable records of sorbent structure samples, test articles, and associated datasets to ensure full traceability.
  • Scientific rigour: You reproduce, document, and validate experimental results in accordance with scientific best practices and established quality standards.
  • Lab operations: You contribute to laboratory management through equipment maintenance, operational support, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement activities.
  • Testing support: You actively support testing campaigns and characterisation activities to ensure timely delivery of project objectives.
  • Safety: You follow and actively promote safe laboratory and workshop practices, fostering a strong culture of safety and compliance.

Requirements

  • Academic background: You hold an advanced degree in materials science, engineering, or a related field.
  • R&D experience: You bring 2–5 years of experience in research and development.
  • Technical expertise: You are skilled in immobilizing sorbents or catalysts in 3D structures and understand how their properties influence system performance.
  • Stakeholder management: You demonstrate excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
  • Hands-on mentality: You take initiative, are results-driven, and work hands-on from laboratory activities to high-level evaluations.
  • Language skills: You are fluent in English.

Nice to have

Industry experience: You bring experience from the catalysis, air filtration, or functional textiles industries. Process expertise: You have experience with gas separation processes. Characterization skills: You are experienced in material and structural characterization techniques.
