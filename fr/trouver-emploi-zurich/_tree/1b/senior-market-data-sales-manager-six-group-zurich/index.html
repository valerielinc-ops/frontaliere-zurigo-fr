Senior Market Data Sales Manager

Zurich | Working from home up to 40% | Reference 8439

SIX is looking for an experienced, commercially driven Sales Executive to grow our Real-Time Market Data business across EMEA. In this quota-carrying role, you will acquire new clients and expand strategic relationships across the financial-services sector. You will position real-time feeds, APIs and cloud-based data solutions that support critical trading and investment workflows. Managing the full sales cycle, you will work closely with Product, Pre-Sales, Account Management and Marketing. This is an opportunity to contribute directly to the growth of SIX Financial Information in a fast-evolving market.

What You Will Do

  • Develop and execute the EMEA sales strategy for real-time market data solutions.
  • Identify and secure new business across banks, asset managers, broker-dealers and fintech firms.
  • Manage the complete sales cycle, from prospecting and solution design to negotiation and closing.
  • Understand client workflows and position tailored data-feed, API and cloud solutions.
  • Collaborate with internal teams while representing SIX at relevant industry events.

What You Will Bring

  • At least seven years of experience in financial technology, market data or capital markets.
  • A minimum of five years of successful quota-carrying enterprise sales experience.
  • Strong knowledge of real-time data, trading workflows, licensing models and cloud delivery.
  • Proven consultative-selling, negotiation, communication and stakeholder-management skills.
  • Experience selling to financial institutions across EMEA and using Salesforce or a similar CRM.
