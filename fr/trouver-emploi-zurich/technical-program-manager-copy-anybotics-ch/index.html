Exigences principales

  • Significant experience leading complex technical programs in robotics, autonomous systems, industrial technology, automotive, aerospace, medical devices or another advanced hardware environment
  • Proven experience shipping a certified, volume-manufactured mechatronic product and leading it through development, industrialization and commercial launch
  • Strong engineering foundation in mechanical, electrical, mechatronic, robotics, systems engineering or a similarly relevant discipline
  • Technical depth to understand system architecture, challenge senior technical specialists and recognise system-level risks without needing to design every solution personally
  • Strong command of program-planning methods, critical-path management, structured product-development processes and disciplined change control
  • Practical experience across product cost, industrialization, manufacturing, supply chain, quality, certification and external technology or manufacturing partners
  • Proven ability to influence senior technical and business stakeholders, resolve competing viewpoints and create alignment without relying on direct authority
