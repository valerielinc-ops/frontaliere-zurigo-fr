Postdoctoral Position in Atomic-Scale Light-Matter Interaction and Spin-Photon Interfaces in Nanographenes

The nanotech@surfaces Laboratory is a dynamic team of quantum nanoscientists exploring the frontier of low-dimensional material systems using cutting-edge scanning probe microscopy.

Your tasks

Within the Werner Siemens Foundation project CarboQuant, you will be in charge of an interdisciplinary research effort exploring atomic-scale light-matter interaction in hybrid chromophore-nanographene systems. Your goal is to understand and engineer spin-photon coupling, single-molecule photophysics and excited-state dynamics. To achieve this, you will combine light-coupled scanning tunneling microscopy techniques: STM-induced luminescence (STML), tip-enhanced photoluminescence (TEPL), and ultrafast lightwave-driven tunneling (THz-STM). You will collaborate closely with partners in the CarboQuant consortium and support the supervision of PhD and master students.

Your profile

We seek a highly motivated experimentalist with a PhD in physics, chemistry, materials science, or a related field, who is eager to take a leading role in an interdisciplinary research project. Candidates should have a strong background in surface science, optics, or molecular photophysics. Prior experience with low-temperature scanning tunneling microscopy is a strong asset; experience with ultra-high vacuum, optical spectroscopy, or ultrafast optics is advantageous. Programming skills in Python are expected. We value scientific curiosity, independence, and initiative, combined with a collaborative spirit and enthusiasm for exploring new scientific frontiers in an international research environment. Excellent communication skills in English are required.

Our offer

We offer the opportunity to take on a leading role in a young research team and to become part of a stimulating and collegial work environment. You will conduct pioneering studies on single-molecule quantum systems using a unique combination of light-coupled scanning tunneling microscopy techniques. You will collaborate with top researchers globally, shaping a competitive research portfolio in a dynamic, interdisciplinary environment. Located in the Zurich area, the Swiss Federal Laboratories for Materials Science and Technology (Empa) offer outstanding infrastructure, a broad interdisciplinary environment and competitive salaries. The position is available starting early 2027 or upon agreement, with a planned duration of at least two years.
