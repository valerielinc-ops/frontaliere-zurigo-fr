Description complète

Your role Interested in working in finance, specifically in global markets, but not sure where to start? Do you want to work for a firm with a truly global footprint?

We're looking for ambitious students to join our 2027 Internship Program within our Investment Banking Global Markets Execution Sales business.

You’ll get to

  • collaborate with our global trading and sales desk on various topics like new product initiatives, front-to-back controls, ad-hoc surveys and trade data analysis
  • work with senior management across the business to analyze, develop, and implement strategic solutions to improve and enhance our business models

Your team You’ll be part of our Global Markets Execution Sales team, based in Zürich. We’re in the center of the trade floor, positioned to support our Global Markets, Wealth Management and Investment Banking sales force. Our team works very closely with senior stakeholders and control functions.

Your expertise

We’re looking for a candidate who

  • has completed at least 4 semesters of a bachelor's degree, and graduated no longer than twelve months ago (bachelor's or master's degree)
  • has previous internship experience, preferably in IB and / Global Markets
  • has strong Microsoft Office skills i.e., Excel, Word and Power-Point
  • has values that align with ours: hard-working, trustworthy, dedicated and collaborative
  • is a strategic thinker with strong communication skills
  • is fluent in English (written and spoken); Italian, French or German fluency are a plus
  • is motivated to work in a business with high demands and tight deadlines
  • can evidence how experimental and responsible use of AI can improve outcomes and efficiency

Start date: February 2027

We’re looking for someone who’s curious and wants to thrive in a business that never stops moving. We welcome all majors, but it’s important to have an interest in the financial industry. We want to see what makes you unique and discover what you can bring to our team. Perhaps you’ve organized a fundraising event or taken part in team sports? Or stepped out of your comfort zone to learn about a subject you found challenging? Let us know how your achievements match the skills we’re after.

Your program This paid internship will last 6 months. You’ll work with our experts and learn about the industry, our clients and our firm. You’ll be right at the heart of our business, learning from your colleagues, taking part in day-to-day operations. You'll be given mentors along the way and will have the chance to get to know some of the most senior members of our teams.

You’ll have plenty of opportunities to develop new skills and make contacts along the way. If you like challenges, enjoy learning and want to work where no two days are the same, this program is for you. If you have a successful internship, you may get an offer to return for our Graduate Talent Program after you've completed your studies.

About us UBS is a leading and truly global wealth manager and the leading universal bank in Switzerland. We also provide diversified asset management solutions and focused investment banking capabilities. Headquartered in Zurich, Switzerland, UBS is present in more than 50 markets around the globe. Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Zurich ?

Les citoyens UE/AELE résidant dans un État UE/AELE peuvent demander un permis G et travailler dans toute la Suisse : les zones frontalières ne s'appliquent plus à eux, et ils doivent rentrer au moins une fois par semaine. L'employeur suisse dépose la demande auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Zurich après signature du contrat. Les délais varient selon le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Zurich ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.
