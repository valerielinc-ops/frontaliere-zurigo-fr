Description complète

Overview Visual Studio Code is where millions of developers turn ideas into working software. We are looking for a Principal Software Engineer in Zurich to tackle hard product and platform challenges as VS Code and GitHub Copilot continue to reshape how developers build software. You will help to evolve VS Code as a market leading developer environment, seamlessly connecting its editor, agentic coding abilities, platform and extension ecosystem. You will identify where investment matters, prove difficult ideas in code, and establish approaches that multiple teams can build on. This is hands-on technical leadership: the opportunity to change both what developers can accomplish and what our engineering teams can deliver.

About the team We work in the open, with direct feedback from developers and extension authors. You will partner with engineers, designers, researchers, and product leaders across our team, GitHub, and Microsoft. We combine community feedback, research, experiments, and engineering judgment to decide what to build. Performance, accessibility, extensibility, security, and developer control are part of the product, not finishing touches.

Examples of work The role spans core VS Code and Copilot; illustrative projects include:

  • Address architectural bottlenecks in large workspaces, proving an approach in code that multiple feature teams can adopt.
  • Define extension or workspace APIs that unlock new capabilities while preserving compatibility with existing extensions and remote development environments.
  • Prototype how developers delegate, review, and validate work with Copilot, then establish the product and platform boundaries needed to ship it.
  • Reshape and support our engineering process with custom solutions fine-tuned for throughput and quality.

Responsibilities

  • Identify significant product opportunities and systemic engineering problems; set technical direction that balances near-term delivery with long-term product and platform health.
  • Prove approaches through prototypes, critical implementation, testing, and difficult debugging, using hands-on work to guide architectural decisions.
  • Lead cross-team delivery across Microsoft and GitHub, resolving architectural disagreements, clarifying ownership and API boundaries, and enabling teams to deliver independently.
  • Own outcomes beyond release, using adoption, quality signals, evaluations, experiments, and user feedback to guide investment and adjust course.
  • Improve engineering effectiveness across teams through testing strategies, performance work, shared design patterns, and tools for rapid, reliable experimentation.
  • Lead technical discussions in the open, investigate community-reported issues, and work directly with developers and extension authors to shape designs.
  • Mentor experienced engineers and technical leaders, helping them develop judgment and take ownership without making yourself a bottleneck.

Qualifications Basic Qualifications

  • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or related technical field AND 6+ years technical engineering experience with coding in languages including, but not limited to, C, C++, C#, Java, JavaScript, or Python OR equivalent experience.
  • Demonstrated technical leadership across multiple teams, including setting direction, building alignment, and delivering substantial initiatives without relying on reporting-line authority.
  • Solid hands-on coding and design skills, with the ability to become productive in large TypeScript and Rust codebases.
  • Experience designing shared platforms, APIs, or large application architectures, with sound judgment about extensibility, compatibility, performance, and maintainability.
  • Solid product judgment, including identifying opportunities, setting priorities amid ambiguity, and turning technical decisions into practical designs and measurable improvements for users.
  • A track record of mentoring experienced engineers and improving the effectiveness of teams beyond your immediate area.
  • Clear communication skills, including explaining complex technical decisions and navigating tradeoffs with engineering and product partners.

Preferred Qualifications We welcome a range of product backgrounds; these are helpful experiences, not a checklist. Previous work on developer tools, open-source projects, or AI products is not required.

  • Master's Degree in Computer Science or related technical field AND 8+ years technical engineering experience with coding in languages including, but not limited to, C, C++, C#, Java, JavaScript, or Python OR Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or related technical field AND 12+ years technical engineering experience with coding in languages including, but not limited to, C, C++, C#, Java, JavaScript, or Python OR equivalent experience.
  • Experience with TypeScript, JavaScript and Rust.
  • Experience evolving interactive products with attention to usability and accessibility.
  • Experience guiding cross-platform architecture, performance improvements, or major migrations in a large application.
  • Experience establishing experimentation and feedback practices that connect engineering decisions to user outcomes.
  • Practical familiarity with AI coding tools and sound judgment about where they help, where they fall short, and how they change development workflows.
  • Experience providing technical leadership on AI-powered products, open-source projects, or extensible applications.
