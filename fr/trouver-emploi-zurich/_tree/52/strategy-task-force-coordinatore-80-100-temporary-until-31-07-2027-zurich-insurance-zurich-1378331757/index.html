Description complète

We are looking for a Strategy Task Force Coordinator to support the Strategy Team within the Group Strategy business unit.

If you are highly organized, flexible, customer-oriented and used to anticipate the needs of your team, then we look forward to receiving your application.

What you will do

  • Provide administrative support for the Executives and team members and manage administrative tasks independently.
  • Executives’ diary management ensuring best use of their time.
  • Support the practical onboarding and offboarding of team members in collaboration with HR and IT.
  • Provide meeting support including coordinating operational aspects of meetings and regular calls and appointments.
  • Organize domestic and international travel arrangements, itineraries, team events and workshops.
  • Manage budget administration, process expenses and invoices, purchase order requests, handle monthly expense reports and general ad-hoc administrative tasks.

What you bring

  • Flexible approach to apply and adapt to different business needs.
  • Excellent organizational skills with customer-oriented personality and strong proactiveness.
  • Independent, but collaborative way of working, hands on approach.
  • Ability to develop and maintain effective working relationships with multiple stakeholders from our worldwide offices.
  • Excellent oral and written communication skills in English, German is an advantage.
