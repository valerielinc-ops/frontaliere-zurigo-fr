Description complète

Are you a passionate P&C reserving actuary looking to take your career to the next level in a truly global, dynamic environment? Swiss Re Corporate Solutions offers you the opportunity to make a real impact — working at the intersection of actuarial excellence, financial innovation, and international collaboration. About the Role

This is an exciting opportunity to join Swiss Re Corporate Solutions as an Actuary within the Global CorSo Actuarial Reserving team. You will play a central role in reserving, economic valuation, and financial reporting, while also contributing to the business unit's ambitious growth strategy. If you thrive on technical depth, cross-functional collaboration, and driving meaningful change — including supporting a major finance IT transformation — this role is made for you. Key Responsibilities

  • Quarterly Reserve Estimation: Lead the quarterly estimation of P&C insurance portfolio reserves (local statutory and IFRS-17), working in close partnership with key internal stakeholders including Claims, Underwriting, Pricing, and Finance/Accounting
  • Pay-out Pattern Estimation: Develop and maintain pay-out patterns that serve as critical input parameters for IFRS-17 cashflows and solvency calculations
  • Financial Reporting: Prepare and present internal and external (statutory) financial reports , ensuring accuracy, compliance, and clarity for a range of audiences
  • Portfolio Reviews & Analysis: Conduct detailed ad hoc portfolio reviews and analyses as required, providing actionable insights to support business decisions
  • Finance IT Transformation: Provide actuarial expertise and support in the ongoing finance IT transformation, helping to shape the future of actuarial processes and systems
  • Stakeholder Collaboration: Build and maintain strong relationships with key internal stakeholders, fostering a culture of transparency, trust, and shared accountability

About the Team

You will become a valued member of the Swiss Re Corporate Solutions Global CorSo Actuarial Reserving team — a diverse, globally distributed group of actuarial professionals united by a shared commitment to technical excellence and innovation. The team is responsible for determining technical reserves at adequate levels, meeting all local statutory and IFRS-17 requirements for Property, Specialties and Credit and Surety direct business lines. Beyond core reserving activities, the team plays a pivotal role in supporting Swiss Re Group across all Non-Life Reserving aspects related to Swiss Re Corporate Solutions. With colleagues spanning multiple geographies and backgrounds, this is a team where diverse perspectives are celebrated, intellectual curiosity is encouraged, and collaboration is at the heart of everything we do.

About You

We are looking for a curious, driven, and collaborative actuarial professional who brings both technical rigour and a proactive mindset to everything they do. You are someone who thrives in a fast-paced, international environment, can manage multiple priorities with confidence, and genuinely enjoys working across different cultures and personal styles. You bring a growth mindset to your actuarial qualification journey and are motivated to continue developing your expertise.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • P&C (Re-)Insurance Experience: At least 3–5 years of experience in P&C (re-)insurance, with a solid foundation in reserving methodologies
  • IFRS-17 & US GAAP Knowledge: Good understanding of IFRS-17 and US GAAP financial principles and their application to reserving for P&C (re)insurance business
  • Actuarial & Statistical Software: Demonstrated experience using actuarial and statistical software tools relevant to reserving and portfolio analysis

These are additional nice to haves

  • Actuarial Qualification: Motivation to deepen and finalize actuarial qualification (e.g., Actuary CAS or equivalent)
  • Credit & Surety Business: Experience with Credit and Surety lines of business is a distinct advantage
  • Communication Skills: Strong interpersonal, written, and verbal English communication skills ; proficiency in additional languages is a welcome plus
  • Prioritisation & Multi-tasking: Proven ability to prioritize, plan, and execute across multiple work efforts simultaneously in a dynamic environment
  • Cross-cultural Collaboration: Demonstrated ability to work effectively with diverse teams across different personal styles and cultural backgrounds
  • Proactive Attitude: A self-starter mentality with the drive to take initiative and contribute beyond the immediate scope of the role
