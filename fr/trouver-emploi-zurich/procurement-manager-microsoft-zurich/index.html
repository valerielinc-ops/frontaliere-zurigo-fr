Vue d’ensemble du poste

We are looking to fill the critical role of Procurement Manager in our datacenter team.You will lead strategic sourcing and supplier management activities across complex construction and infrastructure portfolios, ensuring the timely delivery of critical goods and services while optimizing cost, quality, risk, and schedule performance.

The role develops category strategies, manages competitive sourcing events, negotiates commercial agreements, oversees supplier performance, and drives cross-functional alignment between engineering, construction, cost management, planning, and project delivery teams.

Acting as the commercial interface between internal stakeholders and external suppliers, the Procurement Manager proactively manages market risks, supply constraints, change management, and contractual obligations, while leveraging analytics, performance metrics, and governance frameworks to support business objectives, operational excellence, and project success.

  • Build strategic relationships with suppliers and drive cross-functional engagement, executive-aligned negotiation strategies, and early business involvement to define sourcing solutions, optimize spending, resolve disputes, and support informed decision-making.
