Doctoral Student at Ingenuity Lab

100%, Zurich, fixed-term

Engineering the future of medicine:The Ingenuity Lab at ETH Zurich, led by Prof. Inge K. Herrmann, develops next-generation medical materials and technologies at the interface of engineering, materials chemistry and medicine. Our ambition is to fundamentally rethink how technologies interact with living systems, opening new avenues for precision diagnostics, targeted therapeutics and minimally invasive interventions. We are looking for an exceptionally motivated, creative and scientifically curious doctoral researcher who is excited about addressing unmet clinical needs through engineering innovation. Our research combines fundamental scientific discovery with technology development and clinical translation. We engineer materials and devices that sense, respond to and actively interact with biological environments.

Job description

Depending on the candidate’s expertise and interests, potential research directions include:

  • Intelligent medical materials: Adaptive, responsive and in situ forming biomaterials for therapeutic and surgical applications
  • Next-generation diagnostics: Innovative sensing concepts and spectroscopic technologies for monitoring biological processes
  • Minimally invasive interventions: Materials and technologies enabling precision surgery and robotic interventions
  • Women's health engineering: Transformative diagnostic and therapeutic technologies addressing historically underserved clinical needs

The doctoral project will be embedded in a highly interdisciplinary research environment, with close collaborations across engineering, natural sciences and medicine.

Profile

We are looking for candidates with

  • A Master's degree in physics, mechanical engineering, chemistry or a related discipline
  • An excellent academic track record and strong scientific and analytical skills
  • Intellectual curiosity, creativity and a genuine enthusiasm for experimental research
  • The ability to think independently, challenge established concepts and develop unconventional solutions
  • A collaborative mindset and enthusiasm for working across disciplinary boundaries
  • Excellent communication skills in English

Above all, we value scientific originality, initiative and the ambition to make a meaningful difference.

We offer

  • A stimulating, international and highly interdisciplinary research environment at ETH Zurich
  • Access to state-of-the-art engineering, materials characterization and biomedical research infrastructure
  • Close interaction with clinicians and access to clinically relevant research environments at Balgrist University Hospital, including OR-X
  • Opportunities to translate scientific discoveries into tangible medical technologies, including exposure to intellectual property development and entrepreneurship
  • Individual scientific mentoring, professional development and opportunities to build an international research network
  • Employment conditions and doctoral remuneration in accordance with ETH Zurich regulations

We value diversity and sustainability

In line with our values , ETH Zurich encourages an inclusive culture. We promote equality of opportunity, value diversity and nurture a working and learning environment in which the rights and dignity of all our staff and students are respected. Visit our Equal Opportunities and Diversity website to find out how we ensure a fair and open environment that allows everyone to grow and flourish. Curious? So are we.

We look forward to receiving your application, including

  • A motivation letter outlining your scientific interests, previous research experience and ambitions
  • A curriculum vitae
  • Academic transcripts and degree certificates
  • Contact details of two academic references

Further information about The Ingenuity Lab can be found on our Website . Specific questions regarding the position should be directed to Lisa Cartelli, lcartelli@ethz.ch (no applications).

Please note that we exclusively accept applications submitted through our online application portal. Applications via email or postal services will not be considered.

We would like to point out that the pre-selection is carried out by the responsible recruiters and not by artificial intelligence.

In your motivation letter, we encourage you to briefly describe a medical challenge that you believe could be addressed through engineering innovation and how you might approach it.
