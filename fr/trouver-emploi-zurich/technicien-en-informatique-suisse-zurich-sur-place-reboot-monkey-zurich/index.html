Exigences principales

  • 2+ years of data center or IT infrastructure experience.

  • Strong cabling skills, including Cat5e, Cat6, Cat6a, single-mode and multimode fiber.

  • Professional communication skills in English.
  • Legally authorized to work in Switzerland without employer sponsorship.
  • Familiarity with DCIM platforms, ticketing systems, and major colocation providers is preferred.
