Description complète

Your role Interested in working in finance, and have a passion for technology, but not sure where to start? Do you want to work for a firm with a truly global footprint?

We're looking for ambitious students to join our 2027 Internship Program within our Booking Services team.

You’ll get to

  • develop and implement technical solutions that power our financial systems
  • contribute to shaping the future of banking building modern and scalable event-driven systems
  • join a forward thinking team of experience software professionals where AI agents are not just tools, but trusted teammates

Your team You’ll be working in the Group Technology Office located in Zurich. We’re responsible for sourcing, developing, implementing, maintaining and supporting our world-class technology solutions to help our business partners to succeed. We’re a global organization that is passionate about technology and believe in a culture of openness, collaboration and continuous learning.

Your expertise

We’re looking for a candidate who

  • has completed at least 4 semesters of a bachelor's degree, and graduated no longer than 12 months ago (bachelor's or master's degree)
  • has a competitive minimum GPA of 4.5 according to the Swiss grading system
  • has values that align with ours: hard-working, trustworthy, dedicated and collaborative
  • is a strategic thinker with strong communication skills
  • is motivated to work in a business with high demands and tight deadlines
  • can evidence how experimental and responsible use of AI can improve outcomes and efficiency
  • is comfortable working with or willing to explore AI augmented development in Java and / or Kotlin
  • is interested in how software is built and deployed with CI/CD tools at scale
  • has a basic understanding or willingness to explore Azure Cloud

Start date: 1 February 2027

Your program This paid internship will last six months. You’ll work with our experts and learn about the industry, our clients and our firm. You’ll be right at the heart of our business, learning from your colleagues, taking part in day-to-day operations. You'll be given mentors along the way and will have the chance to get to know some of the most senior members of our teams.

You’ll have plenty of opportunities to develop new skills and make contacts along the way. If you like challenges, enjoy learning and want to work where no two days are the same, this program is for you.

If you have a successful internship, you may get an offer to return for our Graduate Talent Program after you've completed your studies.

Questions sur cette offre

Quel salaire propose UBS pour ce poste ?

L'annonce d'origine n'indique pas de salaire. Notre estimation pour ce rôle et ce secteur à Zürich est de CHF 83'000 - 126'000 bruts par an — une estimation, pas un montant communiqué par UBS. Le calculateur de ce site convertit un salaire brut en net réel une fois l'impôt frontalier et les cotisations sociales appliqués.

Est-ce un poste à temps plein, et quel type de contrat propose UBS ?

Cette offre est un poste en temps plein. Le type de contrat affiché ici provient directement de l'annonce originale de l'employeur ; confirmez toujours les horaires exacts, le préavis et la durée d'essai avec UBS pendant le processus de candidature, ces détails pouvant varier même au sein de la même catégorie de contrat.

Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Zurich ?

Les citoyens UE/AELE résidant dans un État UE/AELE peuvent demander un permis G et travailler dans toute la Suisse : les zones frontalières ne s'appliquent plus à eux, et ils doivent rentrer au moins une fois par semaine. L'employeur suisse dépose la demande auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Zurich après signature du contrat. Les délais varient selon le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Zurich ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.

Comment postuler à ce poste chez UBS ?
