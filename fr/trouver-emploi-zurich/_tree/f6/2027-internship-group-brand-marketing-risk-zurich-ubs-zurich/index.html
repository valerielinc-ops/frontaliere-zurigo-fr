Description complète

Your role Interested in the intersection of finance, brand, marketing and risk, but not sure where to start? Do you want to gain hands-on experience at a firm with a truly global footprint?

We’re looking for ambitious students to join our 2027 Internship Program within Group Brand & Marketing Risk.

You’ll get to

  • gain a front-row view of brand and marketing risk governance by helping prepare decision-ready meeting materials, dashboards and reports, tracking actions and supporting timely escalation
  • help strengthen the controls that protect the UBS brand by supporting process and evidence documentation, reviewing results and following up on exceptions
  • turn risk insights into practical advice by contributing to the Group Brand & Marketing risk profile and focused deep dives across sponsorships and partnerships, channel activation, the UBS Art Collection, sustainability and impact, and UBS branding
  • gain a Group-wide perspective on marketing sourcing risk by supporting oversight and governance of supplier, third-party and commercial risks and contributing to well-informed sourcing decisions across UBS
  • see how risks are managed from identification through resolution by supporting remediation activities, issues, audits and reviews, and helping respond to requests from second- and third-line functions
  • make risk management smarter and more effective by contributing to process and reporting improvements and collaborating with colleagues across Group Brand & Marketing, business divisions, Legal, Compliance and Internal Audit

Your team Based in Switzerland, our team manages non-financial risk across the global activities of Group Brand & Marketing, part of UBS Group Functions. You’ll learn from experienced risk professionals, gain broad stakeholder exposure and help strengthen a sustainable control environment across the firm.

Your expertise

We’re looking for a candidate who

  • has completed at least 4 semesters of a bachelor's degree, and graduated no longer than 12 months ago (bachelor's or master's degree)
  • has a competitive minimum GPA of 4.5 according to the Swiss grading system
  • has values that align with ours: hard-working, trustworthy, dedicated and collaborative
  • is a strategic thinker with strong communication skills
  • is motivated to work in a business with high demands and tight deadlines
  • can evidence how experimental and responsible use of AI can improve outcomes and efficiency
  • is proficient in Microsoft Office, particularly PowerPoint and Excel
  • has excellent German and English language skills, with strong working proficiency in the other language

Start date: 1 February 2027

Your program This paid internship will last six months. You’ll work with our experts and learn about the industry, our clients and our firm. You’ll be right at the heart of our business, learning from your colleagues and taking part in day-to-day operations. You’ll have mentors along the way and the chance to get to know senior members of our teams.

You’ll have plenty of opportunities to develop new skills and make contacts along the way. If you like challenges, enjoy learning and want to work where no two days are the same, this program is for you.

If you have a successful internship, you may get an offer to return for our Graduate Talent Program after you've completed your studies.

Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Zurich ?

Les citoyens UE/AELE résidant dans un État UE/AELE peuvent demander un permis G et travailler dans toute la Suisse : les zones frontalières ne s'appliquent plus à eux, et ils doivent rentrer au moins une fois par semaine. L'employeur suisse dépose la demande auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Zurich après signature du contrat. Les délais varient selon le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Zurich ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.
