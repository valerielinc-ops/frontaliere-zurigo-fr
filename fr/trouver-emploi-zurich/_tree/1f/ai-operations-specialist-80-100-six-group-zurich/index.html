AI Operations Specialist (80-100%)

Zurich | Working from home up to 40% | Reference 8459

SIX is establishing a group-wide AI Enablement Center to accelerate the adoption of artificial intelligence and translate AI opportunities into measurable business, customer and market outcomes. You will work directly with the Head of AI as SIX builds its AI Center. You will combine executive support with business analysis and build small automations that save the team time.

What You Will Do

  • Prepare briefings and presentations, record decisions and follow up on agreed actions.
  • Gather data, compare options and present recommendations to support business decisions.
  • Build and test small automations using AI, such as turning project updates into a draft weekly briefing.
  • Coordinate interviews, onboarding, workshops, demos and team events for the whole AI Center.
  • Manage the Head of AI’s calendar, correspondence, travel and expenses.

What You Bring

  • You communicate confidently in German and English, both in writing and in conversation.
  • You have experience coordinating work across several people. You anticipate what the Head of AI needs, manage competing priorities and follow up on agreed actions.
  • You use AI tools every day for tasks such as research, analysis and drafting. You can explain how you check the accuracy of the results.You follow developments in AI, try new tools and recognise tasks worth automating. You have built and tested a small automation at work, during your studies or in a personal project.
  • You can analyse data, compare options and present a clear recommendation in a short briefing or presentation.
  • You handle confidential information with discretion and know what you can share when using AI tools.
