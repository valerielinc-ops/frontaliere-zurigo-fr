Responsabilités principales

  • Key Responsibilities
  • Take end-to-end ownership of the Position Management and Portfolio Analysis platform, balancing feature delivery, technical enhancement, and production stability.

Exigences principales

  • Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Finance, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • Minimum of 5 years of experience in product ownership or product management, ideally within wealth management, asset management, or investment operations.
  • In-depth understanding of portfolio accounting concepts, including position valuation, performance measurement, accruals calculation, cashflow analysis and transaction processing.
  • Demonstrated hands-on experience with the Temenos Triple A platform is strongly preferred.
  • Proven track record in delivering complex technical projects using Agile methodologies (Scrum, SAFe) and DevOps practices.
  • Experience managing distributed teams in a global, multicultural environment; familiarity with APAC, EMEA, and nearshore delivery models is advantageous.
  • Strong analytical mindset with attention to detail, combined with excellent verbal and written communication skills.
  • Ability to thrive in fast-paced, evolving environments and influence without direct authority.
