Description complète

Founded in 1894, Universal Genève is an iconic Swiss watchmaking Maison celebrated for its rich heritage and timeless elegance. Known as the “Couturier de la Montre”, the Maison unites technical excellence with refined design, crafting timepieces with the same precision, creativity, and attention to detail as a couture creation. Today, Universal Genève enters a new chapter. Its revival is shaped by exceptional timepieces and a commitment to building a distinctive, contemporary expression of the Maison across every touchpoint. Social media is where this new chapter comes to life — connecting the Maison with a global community through storytelling, culture, conversation, and creativity. For those ready to build a legacy Joining Universal Genève at this stage means becoming part of a defining relaunch, with the opportunity to shape how a historic Maison engages with a new generation of clients, collectors, and communities. As Senior Social Media Manager, you will own the Maison’s social media strategy and presence, translating its identity into compelling content while building meaningful relationships with audiences across platforms. Working closely with Brand Communications, Editorial, Creative, Digital, and external partners, you will ensure a distinctive and consistent social presence while using data, cultural insights, and platform developments to continuously evolve the approach.

Your craft Social media strategy & content

  • Own and implement the Maison’s social media strategy across relevant platforms
  • Develop editorial calendars and lead social-first storytelling across campaigns, products, heritage, and brand moments
  • Shape the Maison’s social voice and ensure content is adapted to each platform
  • Identify relevant cultural and platform trends and translate them into opportunities for the Maison Community & engagement
  • Own the community management strategy and ensure meaningful, consistent engagement across channels
  • Monitor conversations, audience behaviour, and sentiment, identifying relevant insights and opportunities
  • Establish clear principles and processes for community engagement and escalations Performance & partnerships
  • Own social media reporting, KPIs, and performance analysis, turning insights into actionable recommendations
  • Coordinate agencies, creators, and external partners to ensure quality and consistency
  • Own and update social media guidelines and best practices across internal and external stakeholders Innovation
  • Stay ahead of social platforms, digital culture, emerging formats, and technologies
  • Continuously test and evolve the Maison’s social media approach What will make you successful
  • 8+ years of international experience in social media, digital communications, content, or a related field, ideally within luxury and premium consumer brands
  • Proven experience developing and implementing social media strategies across multiple platforms
  • Strong understanding of social platforms, content formats, community management, and audience behaviour
  • Excellent project management and stakeholder coordination skills
  • Strong editorial, storytelling, and communication skills
  • Confident working with social media analytics, reporting, and listening tools such as Brandwatch, Hootsuite, Sprinklr, or equivalent
  • Creative, proactive, culturally curious, and highly adaptable
  • Strong ownership and attention to detail, with the ability to work independently
  • Excellent English and French communication skills
