Description originale

Department of Political Science

Start of employment 01.01.2027, temporary

The Department of Political Science at the University of Zurich invites applications for a post-doctoral research position in the research group of Professor Fabrizio Gilardi.

Your responsibilities

  • Teach at the BA and/or MA level (1.5 classes per semester on average, in English or German).
  • Conduct and publish high-quality research.
  • Collaborate with other members of the research group.
  • Provide support for research, organizational, and administrative tasks.

Your profile

  • PhD in political science or a related discipline (completed or close to completion).
  • Research experience relevant to digital technology and politics, broadly defined; interest in public policy is an asset.
  • Ambition and ability to produce high-quality and high-impact research, both independently and in a team.
  • Ability to work constructively and effectively both independently and as part of a research team.
  • Proficiency in quantitative research methods, preferably computational social science, experimental design, and/or causal inference.
  • Very good command of English; working knowledge of German is an asset
